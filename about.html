About the Journal.

AI Love You is an editorial journal exploring design, architecture, fashion, film, and music — the objects, spaces, and ideas worth paying close attention to.

Editor's Note
"We believe the best stories live in the specific: the click of a switch, the cut of a jacket, the concrete of a building. The Journal is our ongoing attempt to look closely at the objects, spaces, and ideas that define a considered life."
Our Editorial Vision

A Curated Archive of Enquiry

Welcome to the Journal. Operating from Denton, Northamptonshire, our mission is to bring critical thought, real sourcing, and a personal perspective to the design, architecture, fashion, film, and music worth paying attention to. This is not just a publication; it is a curated archive of enquiry.

We believe that in an increasingly disposable world, the need for grounded, considered storytelling about the objects and spaces we choose to live with becomes more vital, not less. The Journal serves as a space to pause, reflect, and engage properly with the things that are built — and built well — to last.

Curation & Structure

The Departments

Each edition is a long-form deep-dive, built around a single unifying theme. It blends real research and sourcing with a distinctly editorial narrative, ensuring that every subject is approached with clarity, rigour, and aesthetic care.

Six departments form the core of every edition — Movies, Music, Products, Design, Architecture, and Style & Fashion — with Travel, Vehicles, Food & Drink, Watches, Technology, and Art & Photography drawn in as each theme demands, up to a maximum of seven departments per edition. Every edition closes with the Global Five: five real, dated events from around the world worth knowing about.

New editions publish as each one is ready, rather than to a fixed schedule. They form a growing chronological record of our research and a consistent touchpoint for our readers.
